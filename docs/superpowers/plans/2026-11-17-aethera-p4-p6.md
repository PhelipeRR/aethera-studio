# Aethera P4–P6 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Complete the remaining Aethera production path with explicit real audio assets, a blocking delivery gate, and a reproducible 60-second FFmpeg MP4.

**Architecture:** Keep the renderer as the single FFmpeg compositor. P4 adds a small asset-resolution module and passes verified local music, ambience, and SFX files into the existing filtergraph; generated placeholders remain available only for legacy fixtures and are surfaced in diagnostics. P5 adds a pure delivery gate over FFprobe/QC/audio/caption evidence. P6 exercises acquisition-cache reuse, measured narration, captions, real B-roll, real audio where available, and persisted evidence in one local E2E test.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, Node filesystem/crypto, FFmpeg/ffprobe, existing Cinema IR and Audio-First renderer.

**Spec:** Active Aethera roadmap P4, P5, and P6 objective.

## Global Constraints

- Preserve P0/P0.5, P1, P2, P2.6, and P3 behavior.
- Preserve FFmpeg as the Cinema compositor.
- No paid dependency is mandatory; no new downloads when local artifacts already exist.
- Missing real audio in strict production mode is a technical block, never silently replaced.
- Do not change Scene Grounding thresholds or semantic approval policy.
- Do not discard or overwrite unrelated workspace changes; no push, merge, reset, clean, or destructive deletion.

## Review Focus

- Audio asset missing, invalid, or shorter than the timeline: block or pad with explicit diagnostics, never hide it.
- SFX overlap with speech: preserve ducking and SoundDirector audit.
- Delivery duration and stream mismatch: gate the MP4 even when FFmpeg exits zero.
- Caption coverage shorter than narration: report incomplete word evidence; do not claim full sync.
- Restart/cache reuse: reuse verified local files and re-render only changed artifacts.
